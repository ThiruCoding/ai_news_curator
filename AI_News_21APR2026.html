Google is strategically expanding its AI infrastructure by partnering with Marvell Technology to develop two custom chips – a memory processing unit and a new inference-focused TPU – aiming to complement its existing Tensor Processing Units. This initiative, slated for completion by next year with plans for nearly two million memory processing units, seeks to reduce Google's reliance on Broadcom, its current chip supplier, and associated high per-unit costs, despite a new contract extending their partnership through 2031. The collaboration leverages Marvell's prior experience designing inference chips for Groq, a company whose Language Processing Unit technology was recently licensed by Nvidia for $20 billion, highlighting a broader trend of specialized AI chip development.
