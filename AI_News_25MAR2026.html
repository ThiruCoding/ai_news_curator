Google Research has introduced TurboQuant, a novel compression algorithm alongside Quantized Johnson-Lindenstrauss (QJL) and PolarQuant, designed to significantly reduce the memory footprint of large language models and vector search engines without sacrificing performance. TurboQuant achieves this by employing a two-stage process: high-quality compression via PolarQuant and subsequent error elimination using QJL, ultimately enabling key-value cache compression and faster similarity searches. Experimental results across various benchmarks demonstrate TurboQuant's ability to quantize key-value caches to just 3 bits, achieving up to 8x performance increases on H100 GPUs while maintaining accuracy and minimizing memory overhead.
