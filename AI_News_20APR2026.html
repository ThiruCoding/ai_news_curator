The Swiss AI Initiative, launched in December 2023 with a 20m CHF grant and substantial GPU resources from the Alps supercomputer, represents a major technical undertaking as the world’s largest open science effort for AI foundation models. Backed by a partnership between ETH AI Center and EPFL AI Center, and leveraging the expertise of over 800 researchers across Swiss institutions, the initiative aims to foster frontier AI research. Utilizing the Alps supercomputer's extensive computing power, it will provide open-source software, models, and data to benefit Swiss stakeholders, including SMEs and startups, promoting trustworthy AI adoption. This marks the first initiative of the newly formed Swiss National AI Institute.
