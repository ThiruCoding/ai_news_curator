AI red teaming is emerging as a critical practice for ensuring the security and responsible deployment of generative AI and machine learning models, moving beyond traditional penetration testing to address AI-specific vulnerabilities like prompt injection, data poisoning, and bias exploitation. This process, which simulates adversarial attacks using both manual and automated tools, supports regulatory compliance—including the EU AI Act—and helps organizations proactively identify risks related to fairness, privacy, and reliability. A growing ecosystem of tools, ranging from open-source frameworks like Foolbox and ART to commercial platforms like Mindgard and SPLX, is facilitating continuous security validation and enabling organizations to build resilient AI systems.
