RightNow AI has released AutoKernel, an open-source framework leveraging an autonomous LLM agent loop to automate GPU kernel optimization for PyTorch models, addressing the scarcity of expertise and manual tuning challenges in this complex field. The system, inspired by Andrej Karpathy’s autoresearch project, iteratively edits, benchmarks, and validates kernel code, utilizing a comprehensive 909-line instruction document to guide optimization across various tiers, from block size tuning to advanced algorithms. AutoKernel prioritizes optimization targets based on their impact on overall runtime using Amdahl’s law and incorporates a rigorous five-stage correctness harness to ensure reliability, supporting both Triton and CUDA C++ backends.

Kevin Gu's thirdlayer.inc has released AutoAgent, an open-source library that automates the tedious prompt-tuning loop in agent engineering, drawing inspiration from Andrej Karpathy's autoresearch for ML training. The system utilizes a "meta-agent" to autonomously modify an agent's harness—including system prompts, tools, and orchestration—running benchmarks and iteratively improving performance. Demonstrating its effectiveness, AutoAgent achieved top scores on SpreadsheetBench (96.5%) and TerminalBench (55.1%) within 24 hours, shifting the human role from engineer to director who sets high-level goals. The library's design is plug-and-play, compatible with any benchmark adhering to the Harbor format.
