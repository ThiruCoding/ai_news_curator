NVIDIA researchers have introduced ProRL AGENT, a novel infrastructure designed to overcome bottlenecks in reinforcement learning (RL) training of multi-turn large language model (LLM) agents. Addressing the conflict between I/O-intensive environment interactions and GPU-intensive policy updates, ProRL AGENT decouples rollout orchestration from the training loop via a 'Rollout-as-a-Service' architecture. This decoupling, alongside optimizations like Singularity-based sandboxing and token-in/token-out communication, enables significant performance gains—nearly doubling SWE-Bench scores for Qwen3 models—and facilitates scalable deployment on high-performance computing clusters. The system's design prioritizes hardware efficiency and training stability through asynchronous processing and intelligent resource management.
