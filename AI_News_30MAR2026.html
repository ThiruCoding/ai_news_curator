A recent report by cybersecurity firm Human Security reveals that automated traffic, largely driven by the proliferation of large language models like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude, has surpassed human activity on the internet, growing nearly eight times faster in 2025. This shift, where machine-based traffic now dominates, fundamentally alters the internet's original human-centric design and poses challenges in accurately quantifying bot activity due to unreliable self-identification methods like user-agent strings. While not inherently malicious, this trend, previously around 20% with Google's crawler, is predicted to continue accelerating, with some estimates suggesting AI bots will fully exceed human traffic by 2027, necessitating a focus on establishing trust in machine actions. The report highlights a significant benchmark in the evolving AI landscape of the internet.
