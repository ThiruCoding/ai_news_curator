RightNow AI has released AutoKernel, an open-source framework leveraging an autonomous LLM agent loop to automate GPU kernel optimization for PyTorch models, addressing the scarcity of expertise and manual tuning challenges in this complex field. The system, inspired by Andrej Karpathy’s autoresearch project, iteratively edits, benchmarks, and validates kernel code, utilizing a comprehensive 909-line instruction document to guide optimization across various tiers, from block size tuning to advanced algorithms. AutoKernel prioritizes optimization targets based on their impact on overall runtime using Amdahl’s law and incorporates a rigorous five-stage correctness harness to ensure reliability, supporting both Triton and CUDA C++ backends.

Alibaba's Qwen team has introduced Future-KL Influenced Policy Optimization (FIPO), a novel training algorithm designed to enhance reasoning capabilities in large language models by weighting tokens based on their downstream influence, effectively doubling reasoning chain lengths. This addresses a limitation in existing reinforcement learning approaches where all tokens receive equal reward, leading to performance plateaus. FIPO achieves comparable results to methods using auxiliary models without requiring pre-training on extensive data, and demonstrates improved accuracy on mathematical benchmarks like AIME, with the model exhibiting self-verification behaviors. The team intends to release the open-source training system, though further testing across diverse domains and consideration of increased computational costs are needed.

Kevin Gu's thirdlayer.inc has released AutoAgent, an open-source library that automates agent engineering by leveraging a "meta-agent" to autonomously iterate on an agent's harness—including system prompts, tools, and orchestration—without human intervention. This approach, inspired by Andrej Karpathy's autoresearch for ML training, achieved top scores on SpreadsheetBench (96.5%) and TerminalBench (55.1%) within 24 hours, demonstrating the viability of AI-driven agent optimization and potentially highlighting the benefits of using models within the same family for enhanced performance. The system utilizes a Harbor-compatible task format and Docker containers, making it adaptable to various domains and benchmarks.
