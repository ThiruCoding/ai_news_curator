NVIDIA researchers have introduced ProRL AGENT, a novel infrastructure designed to accelerate reinforcement learning (RL) training for multi-turn large language model (LLM) agents by decoupling agentic rollout orchestration from the training loop. This addresses a critical bottleneck caused by conflicting resource demands – I/O-intensive environment interactions versus GPU-intensive policy updates – through a three-stage asynchronous pipeline and optimizations like Singularity-based sandboxing and direct pseudo-terminal usage. The system demonstrably improves performance, as evidenced by near-doubling SWE-Bench scores with Qwen3 models, and enables scalable deployment on high-performance computing clusters.
