Researchers from Chalmers University of Technology and Volvo Group challenge the narrative that AI agents will replace software engineers, instead arguing they are expanding the field beyond traditional code. They propose a "Semi-Executable Stack" model encompassing six layers—from core code to social factors like the EU AI Act—highlighting the growing importance of engineering "semi-executable artifacts" like prompts and workflows. This shift necessitates a focus on strategic decision-making, validation, governance, and maintenance, revealing a critical gap in engineering methods for the outer rings of the stack, particularly concerning decision routines and institutional fit. Ultimately, the core skill for developers is evolving from building code to strategically managing and governing AI-driven systems.
